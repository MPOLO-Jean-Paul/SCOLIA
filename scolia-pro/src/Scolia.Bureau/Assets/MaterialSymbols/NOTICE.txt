https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

Original SVG geometry is embedded as native Avalonia paths, normalized from the
Google 960-unit canvas to 24 points. catalogue.json records the semantic choices;
manifest.json records the pinned commit, source paths, variants and SHA-256 hashes.
Rebuild with scripts/generer-icones-mobiles.py. The application renders these vectors
offline without downloading a font or an image.

The rounded tile backgrounds, blue and green gradients, highlights and fixed relief
are SCOLIA drawings. Additional existing action shapes retain their Fluent System
Icons provenance and license in Assets/FluentSystem.
