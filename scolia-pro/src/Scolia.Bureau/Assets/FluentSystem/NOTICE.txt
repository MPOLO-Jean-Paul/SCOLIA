Microsoft Fluent UI System Icons
https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-system-icons

Glyphs: official SVG paths on a 24 px canvas, referenced by the existing SCOLIA action identifiers.
Filled variants identify selected radio buttons and solid color swatches.
Color icons: official SVG paths and gradients transcribed to Themes/FluentArtwork.axaml.
The manifest records the source name and SHA-256 of every original SVG downloaded on 2026-11-27.
Source paths: assets/<name>/SVG/ic_fluent_<name>_<size>_<regular|filled|color>.svg
