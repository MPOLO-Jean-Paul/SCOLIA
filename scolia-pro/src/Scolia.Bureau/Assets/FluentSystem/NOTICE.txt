Microsoft Fluent UI System Icons
https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-system-icons

Original regular glyphs on their native 16, 20 and 24 px canvases; optical variants
are selected for small commands without stretching their geometry. Original color geometry and gradients
transcribed to native Avalonia drawings, with chromatic colors adapted to SCOLIA blue
(#1D4ED8) and green (#16A34A), retaining neutral shading and semantic alert colors.
Source commit, paths and SHA-256 of the original SVGs are recorded in manifest.json.
Rebuild with scripts/generer-icones-scolia.py; catalogue.json holds semantic associations.
The application needs no network connection or system icon font to render these assets.
